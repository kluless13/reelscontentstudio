America's most important interest rate just hit a twenty-four-year high. To see why that matters, rewind to nineteen eighty-one.
Back then, the ten-year Treasury yield, what the U.S. pays to borrow for a decade, hit nearly sixteen percent. The Fed was crushing inflation.
Then came a forty-year slide... down, and down...
until twenty twenty, when the pandemic sent it to half a percent.
Then inflation came back. And so did rates. Today it's five point three percent, the highest since two thousand two.
Why care? Mortgages key off this number. A four-hundred-thousand-dollar mortgage now costs about a thousand dollars more a month than in twenty twenty-one.
